## Ranking criteria (weights)
1. Placement strength for finance/quant (spoon-fed-ness, conversion rates, target firms)
2. ROI: years to reclaim ALL cost (tuition + living) at local post-grad salary
3. Chances at GMAT Focus 655 (admission risk)
4. Reclaim-while-studying (part-time work rights + earning potential)
5. Political stability + visa path security (post-study search period)
6. Indian verdict (alumni/forums consensus) + Indian living ease
7. Prestige (global brand, Indian-credential equivalence)
8. Scholarships available to Indians
